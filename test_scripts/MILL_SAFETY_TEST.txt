BEAM SHIFT IS NEVER RESET DURING A MILL (pyFIB v0.55.1, bug #16)            about 25 min
==============================================================================
Why: before v0.55.1 a batch reset the FIB beam shift after every site whatever SmartSEM
read -- also after a mill timeout, and when the pyFIB window died during a mill. A
beam-shift change in the middle of a pattern moves where the rest of it lands. Now the
batch checks the milling state first. Not known: whether SmartSEM applies a beam-shift
write during a pattern at all; step 4 measures that.

Use ONE sacrificial position and a pattern that mills for 3-5 minutes. Rough Mill tab,
Refine ticked (so a beam-shift correction is in use), After-mill image on.

1) MILL TIMEOUT BOX                                                         1 min
   Rough Mill, Prepolish and Polish tabs each show "Mill timeout (min)", default 240.
   ARM OFF, Start: the plan in the Log has "Mill timeout: 240 min per site".

2) A SITE THAT TIMES OUT                                                    8 min
   Command prompt in the pyFIB folder (the tabs do not allow under 10 min):
       python -m pyfib mill-positions <pattern.ely> <position> --positions-file <session>\positions.json --stage rough --refine <position> --wait auto --timeout 90 --arm
   Watch SmartSEM's FIB beam shift X/Y (Status window) from the start.
   Expect after 90 s: "Timeout waiting for milling to finish", then
       "*** The pattern at <position> may still be running in SmartFIB ..."
       "[beam-shift] checking for 30 s that milling has stopped ..."
       "[beam-shift] *** NOT reset after <position>: SmartSEM indicates milling (...)"
   and the pattern keeps milling to its end with the beam shift UNCHANGED.
   Afterwards press Go to for that position: the beam shift reads 0.
   Send: those Log lines and the beam shift before / during / after.

3) THE WINDOW DIES DURING A MILL                                            8 min
   Start the same site from the Rough Mill tab (ARM on). While it mills, end the pyFIB
   WINDOW in Task Manager (the python.exe whose window is pyFIB; not the one with the
   higher PID that runs the batch).
   Expect: the pattern mills to its end; the beam shift is reset only after milling has
   stopped; the after image is saved. Start pyFIB again: it reports the last run ended
   without closing. <session>\runs\run_<time>.log ends with the site finished (with two
   positions ticked: "Batch stopped: the pyFIB window that started this batch is gone ...
   stopped before <position 2>").
   (If the window is ended while a site is still at recall / autofocus / refine, that
   site's mill is not started: "stopped at <position> before its mill was started".)
   Send: the last 30 lines of that run_<time>.log.

4) OPTIONAL, MEASURES THE UNKNOWN: DOES SMARTSEM TAKE A BEAM SHIFT DURING A MILL?  5 min
   Sacrificial area only. Start a 3-5 min pattern by hand in SmartFIB. While it mills,
   change FIB beam shift X by 1 um in SmartSEM. Does the pattern jump, is the change
   refused, or does it apply after the pattern? Send what you saw.

5) ORDINARY BATCH                                                           3 min
   Two positions, everything default. Expect per site, as before:
   "[n/2] FIB beam shift X/Y reset to 0 after <position>." and no "NOT reset" line.
